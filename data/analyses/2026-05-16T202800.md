### ⚠ Engine-state correction (read before any differential)

There is **no engine-restart marker** between `engine_off_key_on`
(t≈1778963670) and `ac_on` (t≈1778963700). Per the operator the engine **was
restarted in that ~30 s gap and was running for the entire accessory sweep**
(`ac_on … passenger_window_up`), shut off at the final `engine_off`
(t≈1778963948). Consequences for analysis:

- The last *automated* engine-state tag before the accessory block is
  `engine_off_key_on`. Any engine-state-aware windowing will mislabel the
  accessory window as KOEO. **Treat `[ac_on, engine_off)` as engine-running.**
- Compute the HVAC / brake / clutch / window byte differentials *within*
  adjacent accessory windows (e.g. `ac_on` vs `ac_off_blower_low`,
  `brake_pedal_pushed` vs `brake_pedal_released`), **not** against the KOEO or
  rev-phase baselines — otherwise the engine-on/off bytes (0x231, 0x284:7/0x285:7,
  0x35D:0, 0x358:1, 0x551:3, …, see 2026-05-13T005522) swamp the accessory
  signal. Done window-locally the missing marker is harmless.
- Warm-idle dwell was short (~90 s between `idle_cold` and `idle_warm`, then
  straight into blips) — this is **not** the still-needed 5–10 min warm-idle
  baseline. The rev/blip phases and the binding sweep are the usable parts.

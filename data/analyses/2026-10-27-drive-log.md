### Predictions (written 2026-10-27 ~15:55Z, before any of today's or the 10-26 data was analysed)

The ECM models the vacuum term of injector ΔP from an assumed rail pressure
and has no rail sensor (`docs/ecu-firmware-analysis.md` injector chain:
pressure factor `w1800`). A rail gauge pressure rising from 48 to 56 psi
(operator's 10-26 note) therefore raises injector flow per pulse by
√((56+v)/(48+v)), where v is manifold vacuum in psi. That is ×1.070 at idle
(v ≈ 7.4), ×1.076 at cruise (v ≈ 3) and ×1.080 at WOT (v ≈ 0). The +1.8 psi
analog-vs-face question doesn't matter here: only the ratio does, and the
48/56 figures come from one instrument.

- **P1 — closed loop, warm (coolant ≥ 80 °C from the capture itself), matched
  rpm × MAF cells.** Total trim (1+STFT)(1+LTFT)−1, mean of both banks, is
  6.5–7.5 % more negative post-pump than pre-pump on the same calibration
  (ZS44A-20261004), with the shift growing slightly with load.
  Pass: median per-cell shift between −4.5 % and −9.5 %, from ≥ 3 cells
  with ≥ 30 reads on each side. Fail: |shift| < 3 % or > 12 %. Unresolved:
  fewer cells.
- **P2 — open loop, enrichment (target φ > 1.05, load-gated, first second of
  each event dropped).** Delivery error λ(PID 0x24)·φ_target − 1 is 7–8 %
  richer post-pump than pre-pump at matched rpm bands.
  Pass: shift −5 % to −10 % with ≥ 20 enrichment reads per side. Unresolved
  below that, which is the expected outcome with no pulls requested.
- **P3 — cold-start / warm-up (open loop before closed loop engages).**
  λ (PID 0x24) sits ~7 % below the target φ implies during open-loop warm-up.
  Unresolved if the warm-up holds < 20 open-loop reads with a target.
- **P4 — LTFT.** Reads exactly 0.00 % on both banks for the whole drive
  (same as 10-26). Fail: any nonzero LTFT read.
- **Not a prediction:** "foggy" exhaust on a cold October morning is
  usually condensed water vapour, and rich exhaust is usually dark or sooty.
  P3 decides whether the warm-up is rich. The fog alone isn't evidence either

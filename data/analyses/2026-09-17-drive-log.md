So when 0x1134 says "17 % more fuel than stoich", the injector and airflow registers say the mixture did not change at all. Delivering φ = 1.17 at 3700 rpm and 105.9 g/s would need ~41,900 PW counts; the register read 36,236.

**But a third instrument says the mixture did change.** Across those same samples the wideband `ECM_AF_SENSOR_V_B1` moved from ~2.00 V (cruise) to **1.18–1.23 V**, decisively in the rich direction, and held there for the whole pull. (This repo has no voltage→λ calibration for that sensor, so the magnitude is not claimed — only the direction and that it is far outside its cruise band.)

Three channels, and they cannot all be right at WOT. The candidates, none yet tested:
1. **The 914,943 counts/g constant does not extrapolate.** It was fit in closed loop at φ ≈ 1 over 0.0061–0.0337 g per injection; these samples are 0.040 g per injection, above that range, and the fit's own note says outside steady state r² falls to 0.79.
2. **`ECM_MAF_GPS` ×0.01 is not linear to ~100 g/s.** A speed-density sanity check makes ~99 g/s plausible at 3700 rpm and this altitude, so it is not obviously wrong, but it was calibrated from steady rungs at much lower flow.
3. **0x1134 is not a mixture command at all** — a load index, an enrichment *request* that was clipped, or a gear/torque term that merely correlates with heavy throttle.
These are separable with one UpRev K-line window during a pull: UpRev logs AFR, MAF and injector pulse width from a different instrument, which pins whichever of the three is lying. That is the cheapest decisive test and it needs the operator.

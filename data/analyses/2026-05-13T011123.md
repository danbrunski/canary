## Tag-order / fan-test caveats

The running `guided_session.py` predated the protocol reorder, so `engine_start`
is tagged at ~t+757 s — **after** the `steer_*`/`brake_*` tags. Per the operator,
the engine was actually **started just before the steering lock test**, i.e.
during the ~136 s `nt510_abs_livedata_start` window (t+567..t+703). So the
`steer_*` and `brake_*` windows are all engine-running, and the cluster of state
bytes that flip in that region are the engine start, not steering — the
steering-window means are clean.

The cooling-fan test was botched tagging-wise: the operator clicked an on/off
control (the ~2.7 s `nt510_fan_test_on`→`_off` window, t+949..t+952) but isn't
sure the fan actually spun; **then**, after exiting that menu, the NT510 offered
a fan-*speed* menu and the operator selected Low then High — which fell **inside
the long `nt510_fan_test_off` window** (t+952..t+1116, ~164 s), untagged for it.
So treat the fan candidate below as unconfirmed; redo with a clean
`fan_low_start/end` + `fan_high_start/end` tag set, ~10–15 s held each.

## Byte-vs-tag differential — candidate signals

`tools/broadcast_signals.py` over the 15 tag windows.

**Steering — 0x002 (the ~100 Hz high-rate ID):** bytes 0 and 1 swing hard and
sweep their full 0–255 range during `steer_full_left`/`steer_full_right`/
`steer_center` and are pinned otherwise — looks like the 16-bit steering-angle
value. Byte 2 goes from ~0 to ~16–28 *only while the wheel is turning* — a
steering-rate / velocity candidate. Strongest steering-correlated ID by a wide
margin. (Caveat: the "pinned" value before steering (≈0xFFD6) differs from after
(≈0x002B), so the sensor reference may not be a simple absolute angle — confirm
against the NT510's ABS steering-angle readout in a follow-up run.)

**Brake — 0x29E:** byte 3 is 0 at rest and averages ~32 over the `brake_press`
window (i.e. ~64–128 during the firm presses, 0 between) — brake-pressure /
pedal-position candidate. Byte 7 sits at ~117 everywhere except `brake_press`
where it jumps to ~149 — a brake-active flag/counter.

**Cooling fan — 0x284 / 0x285 (mirror each other), UNCONFIRMED:** byte 6 reads
~181 over the brief `nt510_fan_test_on` window vs ~127 elsewhere. But the
time series shows bytes 6/7 of 0x284/0x285 *toggle* between 0 and 255 every
~2.5 s — it's a duty-modulated/heartbeat-style field, not a clean value, and
its toggle ratio shifts with engine load (also rose with throttle blips in run
#2). And per the caveat above, the real Low/High fan-speed test wasn't tagged.
So: 0x284/0x285:6 is a *load-proxy* candidate at best; the fan-duty binding
needs a clean redo before it's worth recording.

**State latches:** 0x231 b0/b3/b4 and 0x29E b0 (254→211), 0x625 b3 (157→29),
0x358 b3 (32→0), 0x002 b1 (255→0), 0x551 b3 (160→128), 0x002 b0 (~214→~43) all
flip during the `nt510_abs_livedata_start` window and don't revert — these are
the **engine start** (which happened in that window), so they're engine/ignition
state, not steering- or brake-related.

**Module map:** the 452 s `nt510_module_scan_start` window contains the NT510's
diagnostic request/response traffic to every module (distinct 7Ex-ish ID pairs
per module). Grepping `frames.log` in that window (or
`broadcast_signals --detail-ids`) gives the module → CAN-ID map.

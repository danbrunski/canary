### D01.5 — receive loss persists ⚠️

Measured directly after the drive at warm idle, over a 65 s window, comparing
the kernel's own counter against the daemon's:

| source | frames in window |
|---|---:|
| kernel `can0` `rx_packets` | 120,599 |
| daemon `receive.frames_seen` | 115,480 |
| unaccounted | 5,119 (**4.24 %**) |

`rx_dropped` did not move at all during that window, so this loss is invisible
to `ip -s link` — the same signature as the 6.6 % measured on 2026-09-09. The
two counter reads are not atomic and the daemon's window is the wider of the
two, which biases the estimate low, so **4.2 % is a floor, not a point
estimate**. Some of the original 6.6 % was GIL starvation and is now gone;
what remains needs the dedicated reader thread D01.5 describes.

Separately, `can0 rx_over_errors` rose by **121 during the drive** while
`rx_dropped` stayed flat. Those are MCP2515 controller overflows — only two
hardware RX buffers, and the SPI driver not draining them in time — which is a
driver-level loss underneath the socket-level loss, and a different defect from
D01.5. It should not be folded into the same number.

### What Phase 4 should now do

- **Loss persists → D01.5 is warranted**, and it now has a second, distinct
  cause to separate out (controller overflow vs socket overflow).
- **Stalls did not persist → D01.6 closes** as a symptom of the starved loop.
- **The tick is under budget → D01.4 and D01.7 are not forced.** The feature
  build still holds the event loop for 301.9 ms median, which is the remaining
  argument for D01.4, but it is no longer a deadline argument.
- D01.9 is the highest-value item left: all 31 misses were `compute_busy`.

## Defect found — the Isolation Forest was blind for the entire drive 🔴

`missing_feature_rate` read **1.0** in every snapshot, in every operating mode:
`warmup`, `idle`, `idle_transient`, `city`, `highway`. The active artifact is
`isolation_forest_2026-07-27T045036.161167Z`, trained on eighteen 2026-07-18
to 2026-07-26 sessions, and not one feature it expects was present at serve.

Consequences, in order:

1. **This drive yields no IF detection evidence.** The 76 anomaly events
   emitted in-window came from the behaviour and sequence detectors only.
2. **The 73.0 ms `isolation_forest` stage is time spent scoring an all-missing
   vector**, so it is not a representative cost. It is 22 % of `compute`;
   `sequence` at 251.5 ms dominates either way, so the D01 conclusions above
   stand.
3. Telemetry collection is unaffected, so the session is fully usable for
   retraining.

This is not the model-version mismatch previously recorded — `/status` exposes
no `model_version_mismatches` key on this build, and the manifests load without
a swap rejection. It reads as feature-spec drift between what the serving
pipeline produces and what the July artifact declares. Tracing it needs the
parquet and the artifact's feature list side by side.

## Addendum 2026-09-10 — the blind Isolation Forest, root-caused

Traced without the Pi: the same artifact
(`isolation_forest_2026-07-27T045036.161167Z`) is on the laptop, so the live
feature frame was rebuilt from a stored drive session and compared against the
artifact's declared `feature_columns`.

**Six of its 88 features are not produced at all.**

| absent column | why |
|---|---|
| `decoded:ECM_AIRFLOW_PROXY_23D` + `__mean`, `__std`, `__roc` | channel renamed 2026-08-04 (`c341570`) once UpRev ground truth ruled out airflow |
| `decoded:ECM_CALC_LOAD_B` + `__mean` | binding retracted 2026-08-05 (`b374ba4`); it was the low byte of a 16-bit rpm counter |

The artifact predates both by about a week. The arithmetic is exact and
deterministic: 6 of 88 is **6.8 %** of every row against a
`MAX_MISSING_FEATURE_FRACTION` of **5 %**, so `TrainedIsolationForest.score`
withholds every row, on every tick, permanently. The reproduction on a stored
session reports `rows withheld: 100.0 %` with the per-row missing fraction a
constant 0.068 — matching the drive's `missing_feature_rate: 1.0` exactly.

The margin matters: `ECM_CALC_LOAD_B`'s two columns alone are 2.3 % and would
still have scored. The four `AIRFLOW_PROXY` columns are what clear the ceiling.

**What this actually is.** Both channels were *corrected*, not lost —
`ECM_CALC_LOAD_B` is the retraction AGENTS.md's "never compare aggregates"
rule was written from. Fixing a wrong binding silently blinded the detector
trained on it, and nothing linked a bindings change to the artifacts that
depend on it. A binding retraction is a feature-spec change.

The serving path now says so: `/status.detectors.isolation_forest.structural_blindness`
carries the absent columns, the fraction, the ceiling and a `scores_nothing`
verdict, and the log escalates from "schema reconciled" to an error naming the
consequence. Retraining against current bindings is still owed and is a corpus
decision for the operator. Tracked as D02.5 in
[`docs/roadmap/tasks/archive/D02-drive-findings.md`](../../docs/roadmap/tasks/archive/D02-drive-findings.md).

## Addendum 2026-09-11 — H1's per-channel continuity pass (D02.8)

The gap analysis the drive could not run when the link dropped, now run against
the session parquet on the bench. **It confirms the counters and adds the
detail they could not carry.**

**The fast tier held 1 Hz with no holes.** All fifteen fast-tier channels
landed 1,424–1,448 samples over the 1,498 s window at 0.95–0.97 Hz, with a
median inter-sample gap of exactly **1.00 s**, p99 of **2.1–2.4 s**, and a
worst gap of **3.67 s**. Not one fast-tier channel had a gap over 5 s.

**Complete tuples, on a 1 s grid, over the set the models consume**
(`RPM`, `SPEED`, `ENGINE_LOAD`, `MAF`, `THROTTLE_POS`, `TIMING_ADVANCE`):

| quantity | value |
|---|---:|
| grid seconds | 1,498 |
| seconds with every fast channel present | 1,442 (**96.3 %**) |
| longest incomplete run | **1 s** |

Every one of the 56 incomplete seconds is an isolated single second. There is
no hole in this capture — which is the contrast worth recording, because the
2026-09-09 session this procedure was written for had **~100 s gaps in every
channel** and a concurrent capture with 0 of 468 complete tuples.

**Broadcast CAN was continuous.** 2,439,597 frames at 1,628/s, inter-arrival
median **0.24 ms**, p99.9 5.0 ms, worst **0.17 s**, and **zero gaps over 1 s**.
Note this is the daemon's own view: it says nothing about the ~4.2 % lost at
the socket before the daemon ever sees them (D01.5), which no gap analysis of
delivered frames can detect.

**Slow-tier gaps are by design, not loss.** `COOLANT_TEMP` and the fuel trims
sit at 0.33 Hz with 6 s worst gaps; the 15 s tier (`FUEL_LEVEL`,
`CATALYST_TEMP_*`, `BAROMETRIC_PRESSURE`) shows 30–76 s worst gaps; the Mode 22
DIDs show ~61 s gaps, which is the `default` profile's 60 s DID window
interval. Reading any of those as a defect would be a misreading of the
profile.

H1 was already accepted on the operator's stated bar; this closes the piece
that was owed, and nothing in it changes that acceptance.

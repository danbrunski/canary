**Verdict: consistent, but it cannot confirm the slope today.**

- **The slope and verdict are limited by range, not by disagreement.** Over a 5 kPa span, the 1 kPa steps and GPS noise attenuate the fitted slope (0.91), and the three sub-windows each span only 1–2 kPa. That is why the verdict reads `drifting`.

- **Within this session:** the per-sample fit of ratio vs baro gives −1.6 %/kPa. A ratio proportional to baro would give +1.5 %/kPa. With n = 14 over a 4 kPa span this is noise-dominated, and it cannot confirm anything on its own.
- **Against 80 kPa (context, different day):** the steady closed-loop ratio on 2026-09-17 was **63,808–63,902** (idle 63,325–63,389). If the register were pressure- or density-based, at 63–68 kPa it would read about 63,850 × 65/80 ≈ **51,900** (−19 %). This session reads 62,800 (−1.7 %), and idle reads about 63,700 (+0.5 % against 2026-09-17's idle). Each figure is a per-sample same-instant ratio, but the comparison crosses two days with different IAT: 31–38 °C here, unknown there. **A register proportional to baro is falsified; the air-charge (MAF-mass) reading survives a 12–17 kPa baro difference.**
- **To make it within-session:** poll 0x1208 at hybrid-profile rates (about 2 Hz) through the full 80 → 65 kPa climb. That would give hundreds of steady idle and cruise pairs per 1 kPa bin, instead of 14 in total.

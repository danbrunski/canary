## What happened

Python-can's slcan parser does `dlc = int(string[4])` (decimal!) at
`can/interfaces/slcan.py:282`. Slcan encodes DLC as hex; classic-CAN values
0-8 happen to render identically in both bases so it normally works, but
**any garbage in the DLC position** — bus error, EMI hit, USB jitter — yields
this exact crash and used to kill the whole recording.

Fixed at this session by wrapping `bus.recv()` with a `ValueError`/`CanError`
handler that counts and drops the bad frame, surfaces it via
`RecordingResult.parse_errors` and `manifest.parse_errors`, and logs a warning
on first occurrence + every 100th thereafter. See commit `cf4bca0`.

CANary-HAT assembly notes

SCOPE
- Assemble all 38 SMD parts (144 pads), EXCEPT R7 and R9 - see DNP below.
- DO NOT FIT R7 or R9. Their footprints are on the board deliberately, but
  they are duplicate I2C pull-ups that the host board already provides.
  Leaving them out is intended, not an omission.
- DO NOT SOURCE OR FIT J1 (2x20 stacking header). We supply and fit it
  ourselves. It is the only bottom-side part, so no bottom-side placement
  operation is needed at all.
- Remaining through-hole is 5 parts / 14 joints: J2 (6), J3 (2), J5 (2),
  C2 (2), J4 ground posts (2). Please quote separately. C2 is operator-
  sourced - land pattern only, do not fit.
- J4 (SMA) is mixed technology: SMD body plus 2 through-hole ground posts.
  PLEASE SOLDER THE J4 GROUND POSTS even if the other through-hole parts are
  excluded - it is a board-edge connector that takes cable load, and the SMD
  pads alone are not enough to hold it.

FINE-PITCH PARTS - PLEASE CHECK PIN 1 ORIENTATION
U5 (BMP384, LGA-10, 0.5mm pitch), U6 (LSM6DSOX, LGA-14, 0.5mm pitch) and
U7 (MAX-M10M-00B, 18-pad LCC module) use third-party library footprints whose
zero-rotation convention may not match IPC-7351. If the centroid rotation looks
inconsistent with the assembly drawing, please query it rather than assume the
position file is correct. Rotation error on these three is the most likely
defect on this board. No BGA or QFP parts are present.

DO NOT SUBSTITUTE - these three fail silently, board will look correct but not work
1. Y1 = ABLS2-40.000MHZ-D4YF-T. The "F" specifies FUNDAMENTAL mode. At 40MHz the
   ABLS2 standard product is 3rd overtone, which will not oscillate in this
   circuit. Do not order by frequency/package alone. NOTE the KiCad footprint is
   named XTAL_ABLS2-12.000MHZ-D4Y-T - that name is wrong, the part is 40MHz.
2. U1 = CAT24C256WI-GT3 (onsemi specifically). Design relies on this part's
   internal pin pull-downs. Microchip 24LC256 is NOT a valid substitute.
3. D2 AND D5 = 1N4148W, SOD-123. Both must be SILICON, not Schottky. D5 blocks
   reverse leakage into an unpowered VSUP; D2 blocks reverse current into BT1,
   which is a NON-RECHARGEABLE primary cell with no charge path on the board.
   Schottky leakage would trickle-charge it. These are now the same part in the
   same package - one line item, quantity 2.
   (D2 was a BAT54 in SOT-23 on the 2026-08-14 quote. That is superseded.)

If any of the above is unavailable, please contact us before substituting.

OTHER
- Voltage and power ratings on the BOM are deliberate, not defaults. In
  particular the 100n 0603 capacitors are ALL specified 50V (one of them sits on
  the 12V rail), and both 1k 0603 resistors are specified 0.2W (one dissipates
  ~150mW in a fault condition). Please do not down-rate any line to a cheaper
  equivalent.
- JP1 is a solder jumper (PCB feature), not a component. Leave unpopulated/open.
- FID1-FID3 are fiducials, MH1-MH4 are mounting holes. Not components.
- Surface finish: U5 and U6 are 0.5mm-pitch LGAs. If your process would prefer a
  different finish than the one ordered for reliable placement on these, please
  advise before starting.
